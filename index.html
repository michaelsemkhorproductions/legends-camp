The story

From the hills of Asturias, where the journey began for David Villa, Santi Cazorla, Quini and Juan Mata, to the beautiful beaches of Sarasota.

This Thanksgiving, the historic Valdesoto club brings The Legends Camp international soccer experience to our backyard — giving young players the opportunity to train directly with coaches who have won some of the game’s greatest trophies.

Designed for girls and boys U8–U17, the camp features groups of just 12 players, with personal attention, meaningful feedback and plenty of time on the ball. Players rotate between David Chorro, Angelo Orlando, Chonto Herrera and Marcelo Gonçalves, learning from all four coaches and their different experiences, styles and approaches to the game. Goalkeepers receive specialized coaching with Juan Calatayud.

Across five days of training and one final day of matches, players will develop their technique, decision-making, creativity and teamwork, then put those skills into action. Training takes place on turf, with one special training day on beautiful Siesta Beach bringing a different setting, fresh challenges and a memorable shared experience.
