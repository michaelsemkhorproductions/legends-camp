The camp

A small group A big opportunity

Give your player the chance to train directly with coaches who have won some of soccer’s greatest trophies. With a maximum of 12 players per group, there is room for personal attention, meaningful feedback and plenty of time on the ball.

Field players rotate between David Chorro, Angelo Orlando, Chonto Herrera and Marcelo Gonçalves, learning from all four coaches and their different experiences, styles and approaches. Goalkeepers receive specialized coaching with Juan Calatayud.

Across five days of training and one final day of matches, players work on their technique, decision-making, creativity and teamwork, then put those skills into action. Training takes place on turf, with one special training day on beautiful Siesta Beach bringing fresh challenges and a memorable shared experience.
