Meet the five trophy-winning coaches

Field players rotate through the four field coaches; goalkeepers train with Juan Calatayud.

  • Angelo Orlando

    UEFA Cup winner · Inter, 1994

    Scanning before receiving and using the first touch to escape pressure.

  • Chonto Herrera

    Copa Libertadores winner · Atlético Nacional, 1989

    Selección de Colombia · World Cups 1990 & 1994

    One-on-one defending, overlapping opportunities and recovery positioning.

  • David Chorro

    UEFA Champions League & Copa del Rey winner · Real Madrid training staff, 2014

    Acceleration, balance and match-day warm-ups.

  • Marcelo Gonçalves

    Copa América & Confederations Cup winner · Brazil, 1997

    Seleção Brasileira · World Cup 1998 Finals

    Anticipating passes and deciding when to step forward or drop back.

  • Juan Calatayud – Goalkeeper

    Clean sheet · Hercules CF vs Messi’s FC Barcelona, 2010

    Hungarian league champion · Videoton, 2015

    Goalkeeper positioning, one-on-one decisions and distribution.

The camp

A small group A big opportunity

Give your player the chance to train directly with coaches who have won some of soccer’s greatest trophies. With a maximum of 16 field players and 8 goalkeepers per group, there is room for personal attention, meaningful feedback and plenty of time on the ball.

Across five days of training, players work on their technique, decision-making, creativity and teamwork, then put those skills into action. Training takes place on turf, with one special training day on beautiful Siesta Beach bringing fresh challenges and a memorable shared experience.

Good to know

Still have a question? Email us or text 941-725-5881.

Who is this for?

Girls and boys, ages U8–U17 (born 2010–2018).

When is the camp?

November 21–25, 2026: five training days from Saturday to Wednesday.

Where does training take place?

Robert L. Taylor Community Complex, 1845 34th St, Sarasota, FL 34234. Training is on turf, with one special session on Siesta Beach.

What time are sessions?

Sat–Sun 9:00am–12:00pm. Mon–Tue–Wed 9:00am–4:00pm, with a lunch break 12:00–1:00pm.

What is for lunch?

Due to allergy and dietary concerns, players should pack their own lunch. No food will be provided by the camp. Beverages and hydration will be provided throughout the camp.

What does it cost?

$495 per player, camp jersey included. Optional extended care is $100 per player.

How big are the groups?

Field players: a maximum of 16 per group, with an assistant coach; groups rotate through the four field coaches. Goalkeepers: a maximum of 8 per group, with an assistant coach, training with Juan Calatayud.

How are players grouped?

By age, using the birthdate and current team age group you give at registration. Field players and goalkeepers train in separate groups.

What is extended care?

An optional $100 per player for Mon–Tue–Wed (Nov 23–25): drop-off from 7:30am and pick-up until 5:30pm, around the 9am–4pm sessions. Choose it when you register.

Is there a beach day?

Yes, one training day is on Siesta Beach. Text us at 941-725-5881 for the day, meeting point and transport arrangements.

Who runs the camp?

The Legends Camp is presented in collaboration with Valdesoto C.F., Lakewood Ranch FC, USM, Train Elite and CH4 Soccer Academy. Questions go directly to Jeffrey Apple: jeffrey@thelegendscamp.com or text 941-725-5881.
